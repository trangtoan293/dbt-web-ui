repository describe compatibility or the upstream software. They do not grant
permission to use any dbt Labs mark or logo.

`dbt-craft`, `dbt-studio`, and related strings in the current source are
historical or temporary internal identifiers, not an approved public brand.
Before a promoted release, maintainers must:

1. choose and clear an independent product and repository name;
2. replace public-facing names, package metadata, image coordinates, paths, and
   domains;
3. remove dbt Labs logo assets unless written permission covers their use;
4. keep only truthful compatibility references and the non-affiliation notice;
5. review the current [dbt Labs Trademark Policy](https://www.getdbt.com/dbt-assets/dbt-trademark-guidelines)
   and obtain legal advice appropriate to the publisher.
